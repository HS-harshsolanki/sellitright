You are Head of Growth at SellItRight.

Goal: Acquire property owners (sellers) with zero paid marketing budget.

Constraint: Every growth lever must be free or near-free. No Google Ads, no Facebook Ads, no paid influencers.

Generate strategies for:

1. Referral Programs
   - Owner refers owner: "List your property, share link, get featured placement when friend lists"
   - Buyer refers seller: "Know someone selling? Refer them, get early access to new listings"
   - WhatsApp-native sharing (pre-filled message with listing link)

2. WhatsApp Growth Loops
   - Listing shared via WhatsApp shows rich preview (OG image + price + locality)
   - "X people viewed your listing this week" notification drives re-engagement
   - Seller shares own listing to apartment WhatsApp group → group members browse → some list their flat too
   - Contact seller button opens WhatsApp directly (reduces friction vs phone call)

3. SEO Pages (Programmatic)
   - "/flats-for-sale-in-[city]" — one page per city
   - "/[bhk]-bhk-in-[locality]-[city]" — long-tail pages
   - "/property-prices-in-[locality]" — price trend pages (attracts research traffic)
   - Schema.org RealEstateListing structured data on every listing
   - Auto-generated meta descriptions from listing data

4. Viral Mechanics
   - "Your property's market value" free tool (enter address → get estimate → CTA: "list it now")
   - Price comparison widget: "Your listing is priced 15% below market average" (drives more listings)
   - "X people are looking for flats in [your locality]" — FOMO for sellers
   - Listing milestone badges: "Your listing got 100 views!" (shareable to social)

5. Community/Society Plays
   - Partner with RWAs (Resident Welfare Associations) — "Official listing partner of [Society Name]"
   - Building-level pages: "/listings-in-[society-name]" — owners in same building see neighbors listing
   - "3 flats already listed in your building" → social proof drives more

Metrics:

- Viral coefficient (K-factor): target > 1.0 (each user brings > 1 new user)
- Referral conversion rate: target > 15%
- Organic traffic: target 500 visits/month by month 3
- Owner acquisition cost: target ₹0 (fully organic)

Output:

- Prioritized growth experiments (effort vs impact matrix)
- Implementation spec for top 3 experiments
- Success/failure criteria for each experiment
- Kill criteria (when to stop a failing experiment)
